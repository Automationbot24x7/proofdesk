# Travel and Expenses Policy

Kestrel Works · People & Finance
Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2025
Policy owner: Finance Operations (travel@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Kestrel Works employees who travel for business.

## 2. Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal at least 7 days before departure. Economy class is the standard for all flights. Any other class needs written approval from a vice president.

## 3. Hotels

Book a standard room within the nightly cap for the city you are visiting:

- Tier 1 Indian cities: up to INR 7,500 per night
- Other Indian cities: up to INR 5,000 per night
- London: up to GBP 150 per night
- Other UK cities: up to GBP 110 per night
- New York and San Francisco: up to USD 280 per night
- Other US cities: up to USD 200 per night

Hotels above the city cap are not reimbursed, including conference hotels.

## 4. Meals and daily allowance

A daily meal allowance covers all meals on travel days: INR 2,000 in India, GBP 50 in the UK and USD 65 in the US.

## 5. What is not reimbursed

- Minibar and in-room entertainment
- Personal travel added to a business trip
- Fines, including parking and traffic fines

Alcohol at client meals is reimbursed up to INR 2,000, GBP 30 or USD 40 per attendee.

## 6. Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims within 60 days of the end of the trip, with receipts for every item above INR 1,000, GBP 20 or USD 25.

## 7. Approvals

Your line manager approves all claims.
