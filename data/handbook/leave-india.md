# Leave Policy: India

Kestrel Works India Private Limited · People Team
Version 2.0 · Effective 1 April 2026
Policy owner: People Team India (people.india@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers employees of Kestrel Works India Private Limited in Bengaluru and Pune. Employees in the United Kingdom and the United States follow their own country's leave policy.

## 2. Earned leave

Employees earn 18 days of earned leave in each leave year (1 April to 31 March), accrued at 1.5 days per month. Request earned leave in the HR portal at least 5 working days ahead for absences of more than two days.

## 3. Carrying leave forward

You can carry forward up to 30 days of unused earned leave into the next leave year. Earned leave above 30 days lapses on 31 March. Lapsed leave is not paid out, except as required by law when you leave the company.

## 4. Sick and casual leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave and 6 days of casual leave in each leave year. Sick and casual leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate is needed for sick leave of more than three consecutive days.

## 5. Parental leave

Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents, receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within six months of the birth or adoption.

## 6. Public holidays

Each office publishes 12 public holidays for the calendar year in December of the year before.
