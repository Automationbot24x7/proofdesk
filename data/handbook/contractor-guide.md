# Contractor Guide

Kestrel Works · Vendor Management
Version 1.1 · Effective 1 May 2026
Owner: Vendor Management (vendors@kestrelworks.example)

## Who this covers

This guide covers independent contractors and agency contractors who work with Kestrel Works teams in any country. Employees follow the employee policies instead.

## Working location and hours

Your statement of work sets where and when you work. Remote work, including work from another country, is allowed when your statement of work permits it. Tell your Kestrel Works contact before working from a new country.

## Time off

Contractors do not receive paid leave from Kestrel Works. Tell your Kestrel Works contact about planned time off at least two weeks ahead.

## Expenses

Contractors claim travel expenses only when the statement of work allows it, through their own invoice, at the rates in the statement of work. Contractors cannot use the employee expense tool.

## Equipment

Contractors use their own equipment unless their statement of work provides a company laptop. Contractors do not receive the home office stipend.
