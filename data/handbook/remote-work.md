# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Works · People Team
Version 1.4 · Effective 1 February 2026
Policy owner: People Team (people@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers employees of Kestrel Works in all countries. Contractors agree their work location in their statement of work, and this policy does not apply to them.

## 2. Hybrid working

Employees work from their home office location on at least two anchor days each week, chosen by their team. Other days can be worked from home. Teams publish their anchor days on the team page.

## 3. Working from another country

Employees may work from a country other than their employment country for up to 20 working days in a calendar year. Each stay needs approval from your line manager and from the People Team, requested in the HR portal at least 10 working days before you travel. The People Team checks visa, tax and data rules for the destination and may decline a request. Working from a country where Kestrel Works restricts data access, listed on the security page, is not permitted.

## 4. Fully remote roles

A role becomes fully remote only when the job offer or a written contract change says so. A fully remote arrangement needs approval from the department head and the People Team. Managers cannot approve fully remote work on their own.

## 5. Home office stipend

Employees receive a one-time home office stipend in their first three months, paid with payroll:

- India: INR 15,000
- United Kingdom: GBP 300
- United States: USD 400

The stipend is not available to contractors or interns.

## 6. Core hours and availability

Be available during your team's core hours, which overlap for at least four hours with your manager's working day. Show your working hours and location in your calendar.
