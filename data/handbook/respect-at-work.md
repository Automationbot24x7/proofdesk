# Respect at Work: Raising a Concern

Kestrel Works · People Team
Version 1.0 · Effective 1 March 2026
Policy owner: People Team (people@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers everyone who works at Kestrel Works, including employees, interns and contractors, in every country.

## 2. What we expect

Everyone is treated with respect. Shouting at, belittling, threatening or repeatedly excluding a colleague is bullying, whoever does it and whatever their role.

## 3. How to raise a concern

You can raise a concern about bullying, harassment or discrimination with any member of the People Team, in person, by email to people@kestrelworks.example, or through the confidential form in the HR portal. You do not have to raise it with your manager first, and you should not if the concern is about your manager.

## 4. What happens next

A People Team partner contacts you within two working days, agrees with you how the concern will be handled and keeps you informed. While a concern is being looked at, the People Team can agree temporary changes with you, such as a different reporting line or a change to where you work. These changes are agreed by the People Team, not by the manager the concern is about.

## 5. Support

The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling at any time. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary matter.
