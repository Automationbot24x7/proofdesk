Kestrel Works India · People Team
Version 1.2 · Effective 1 June 2026

## Before your first day

You will receive your laptop by courier at least two working days before you start. Sign in with the temporary password sent to your personal email, then set up multi-factor authentication on your phone.

## Your first day

Arrive at the Indiranagar office by 9:30. Reception will give you your access card and take you to your team. Your manager books a welcome lunch in your first week.

## If your laptop has not arrived

Contact the IT Service Desk at it-help@kestrelworks.example. If you cannot reach them before 10:00 on your first day, call Ravi Menon, our office coordinator, on his personal mobile +91 98450 12345 or write to him at ravi.menon.home@personalmail.example. Ravi lives near the office, at 14 Hosmar Layout, 2nd Cross, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, and can bring a loan laptop.

## Your first month

Complete the security awareness course and the code of conduct course in the learning portal within 30 days. Claim your home office stipend in the HR portal within your first three months.
