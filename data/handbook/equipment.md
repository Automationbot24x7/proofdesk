# Equipment and Devices

Kestrel Works · IT and Finance
Version 2.0 · Effective 1 March 2026
Owner: IT Service Desk (it-help@kestrelworks.example)

## Laptops

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every 36 months, or earlier if IT confirms a fault that cannot be repaired. Request a replacement in the IT portal; you do not need manager approval for a scheduled replacement.

## Monitors and accessories

Employees can request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset for home working through the IT portal. A second monitor needs manager approval. Items stay the property of Kestrel Works.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours, even outside working hours. IT will lock the device remotely. A police report is needed for stolen devices.

## Personal devices

You may read email and chat on a personal phone once it is enrolled in the company device management app. Personal laptops must not be used to access company systems.

## Leaving the company

Return all equipment on or before your last working day. Unreturned equipment is deducted from your final pay.
