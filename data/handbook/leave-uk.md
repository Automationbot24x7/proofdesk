# Holiday and Leave Policy: United Kingdom

Kestrel Works UK Ltd · People Team
Version 2.2 · Effective 1 January 2026
Policy owner: People Team UK (people.uk@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers employees of Kestrel Works UK Ltd in Manchester and London.

## 2. Annual holiday

Employees receive 25 days of paid holiday each holiday year (1 January to 31 December), plus UK bank holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

## 3. Carrying holiday over

You can carry over up to 5 days of unused holiday into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, after which they are lost.

## 4. Sickness absence

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A fit note is needed for sickness absence of more than seven calendar days. Company sick pay is paid at full salary for up to 20 working days in a rolling twelve months.

## 5. Family leave

Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of full company maternity pay. Other parents, including adoptive parents, receive 6 weeks of full company pay.
