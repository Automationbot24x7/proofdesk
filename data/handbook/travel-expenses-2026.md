# Travel and Expenses Policy

Kestrel Works · People & Finance
Version 3.0 · Effective 1 April 2026
Policy owner: Finance Operations (travel@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Kestrel Works employees in India, the United Kingdom and the United States who travel for business. Contractors follow the expense terms in their statement of work and are not covered by this policy. Version 3.0 replaces Version 2.1 in full.

## 2. Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible. Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class needs written approval from a vice president before booking.

## 3. Hotels

Book a standard room within the nightly cap for the city you are visiting:

- Tier 1 Indian cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune): up to INR 9,000 per night
- Other Indian cities: up to INR 6,000 per night
- London: up to GBP 180 per night
- Other UK cities: up to GBP 130 per night
- New York and San Francisco: up to USD 320 per night
- Other US cities: up to USD 220 per night

Conference exception: when you attend a conference, you may stay at the official conference hotel above the city cap, provided you book through the conference room block and your manager approves the booking in the travel portal before you book. Keep the conference booking confirmation with your claim.

## 4. Meals and daily allowance

A daily meal allowance covers all meals on travel days: INR 2,500 in India, GBP 60 in the UK and USD 75 in the US. Receipts are not required for the daily allowance. Client meals are claimed separately with itemised receipts and the names of the attendees.

## 5. What is not reimbursed

- Alcohol, including at client meals
- Minibar, in-room entertainment and spa charges
- Personal travel added to a business trip, including extra hotel nights
- Fines, including parking and traffic fines
- Upgrades booked without the approval required in section 2

## 6. Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the end of the trip, with an itemised receipt for every item above INR 500, GBP 10 or USD 15. Claims submitted after 30 days need approval from your department head and may be rejected. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 7. Approvals

Your line manager approves claims up to INR 1,50,000, GBP 2,000 or USD 2,500 per trip. Larger claims also need approval from Finance Operations.
