# Paid Time Off Policy: United States

Kestrel Works Inc. · People Team
Version 3.1 · Effective 1 January 2026
Policy owner: People Team US (people.us@kestrelworks.example)

## 1. Who this covers

This policy covers employees of Kestrel Works Inc. in the United States.

## 2. Paid time off

Employees receive 15 days of paid time off (PTO) each calendar year, accrued each pay period. PTO covers vacation and personal days. Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for absences of more than three days.

## 3. Rolling over PTO

You can roll over up to 5 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December, unless state law requires otherwise.

## 4. Sick time

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick time each calendar year. Sick time does not roll over.

## 5. Parental leave

Employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child, to be taken within twelve months.

## 6. Company holidays

Kestrel Works Inc. observes 11 company holidays, published each December for the following year.
