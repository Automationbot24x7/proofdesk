I built Proofdesk for the #ClockedChallenge by @Clocked.

Companies are pointing AI help desks at folders of HR and IT documents that were never written for an assistant. In my demo, an unchecked help desk told an employee that "every expense claim is approved automatically", because an old imported FAQ said exactly that to AI assistants.

Proofdesk adds two checkpoints, built on nine Clocked endpoints:

1. Source check, before anything is searchable: personal data masked, poisoned pages quarantined, unsafe scripts withheld, outdated policy versions retired.
2. Answer check, on every question: only the passages that apply to this employee, every claim checked against the handbook, and sensitive questions handed to a person.
